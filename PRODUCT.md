# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Egyptian coffee enthusiasts discovering premium Egyptian coffee. They are culturally proud, taste-conscious, and seek a brand that honors Egyptian coffee heritage while feeling contemporary. Their core job: learn the Galal story, explore the collection, and find a coffee that feels like home.

## Product Purpose

Galal Coffee is the digital flagship of Egypt's most iconic coffee brand. It exists to tell the story of a coffee car on the streets of Port Said in 2015 that grew into a nationwide institution and to make that story, and that coffee, accessible to a new generation. Success is measured in emotional connection first, purchase intent second.

## Brand Personality

**Royal, rooted, warm.** Galal carries the confidence of a king without arrogance. It is deeply Egyptian — not heritage as costume, but heritage as inheritance. The warmth comes from hospitality: coffee is shared, poured generously, never rushed.

## Anti-references

- Generic startup SaaS: no AI-purple gradients, no three-feature-card templates, no tech-brand aesthetics, no "seamless / empower / supercharge" copy.
- Western hipster coffee: no distressed typography, no kraft-paper textures, no Portland/Williamsburg indie aesthetic.
- Mass-market corporate: no Starbucks-clean polish, no stock-photo perfection, no globalized anonymity.

## Design Principles

1. **Heritage as inheritance, not costume.** Every visual reference to Egypt (materials, patterns, language) must feel authentic and earned, not decorative or tourist-facing.
2. **Editorial restraint.** Inspired by Aesop's typographic discipline: let the coffee, the story, and the photography lead. Design recedes; the brand advances.
3. **Warmth through craft, not color.** The palette is dark, moody, rooted. Warmth comes from the photography, the type, and the language — not from a cream background.
4. **Royal confidence, not shouting.** The brand is "The King of Egyptian Coffee." The design carries that weight through composition, scale, and negative space — never through loudness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA compliance. Site already honors reduced motion via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Continue to maintain contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, and legible typography. The dark editorial palette requires vigilance on body text contrast against near-black backgrounds.
